## What a user sees
Typing a very large quantity (99999999999999999999) for the mug is accepted; the cart shows "Total: $1.2e+21" and "Cart (100000000000000000000)".

## Why it matters
Money shown in exponent notation and nonsense quantities reach the cart and checkout. There is no upper limit on quantity at all (1000000 is also accepted).

## Steps
1. Sign in, add Ceramic mug to the cart
2. Set its quantity to 99999999999999999999 and press "Update" (or POST /cart/update productId=1 qty=99999999999999999999)
3. View /cart

## Expected
A valid quantity and a valid money total; no exponent notation (source: CART-005)

## Actual
"Total: $1.2e+21", header "Cart (100000000000000000000)", quantity box 100000000000000000000. qty=9007199254740993 gives "$108086391056891904.00" (precision lost).

## Evidence
evidence/002/v1-c2t1.cart.html · v1-c2t2.cart.html · v1-c2t3.cart.html · t2-cart-b.txt

## Prompt for your coding agent
/cart/update in server.js accepts any positive number as qty. Validate it as a safe integer within a sensible maximum (the open question CART-009 decides whether the cap is the product's stock). Reject or clamp larger values. Add a test that qty=99999999999999999999 doesn't produce "e+" in the cart total.
