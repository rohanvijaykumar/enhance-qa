# Mode: area (a focused check of one feature)

**When:** the user names a feature or area: "/enhance-qa checkout", "QA the export feature", "test login properly".

## Scope

1. **Match the name to an area file** (title, `Area` prefix or `Paths`). If none matches, ask the scout to map it (Job 3) and create the area first.
2. **Every non-retired check in that area,** plus smoke for the intake gate.
3. **Checks in other areas that share its paths** or that the area's `Notes` name as dependent.
4. **Two exploratory charters** at the balanced tier:
   - one from the area's highest-risk tour (money, data or bad-neighbour);
   - one interruption-and-garbage tour.
5. **The relevant feature packs:** if the area uses one that isn't in the plan (it uploads files, for example), the scout adds that pack's checks first.

## Process

The `check` phases (`modes/check.md`), with this scope. Fix per autonomy. The report focuses on the area: its checks, its open bugs, its coverage, and what wasn't covered.
