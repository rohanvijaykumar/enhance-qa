## What a user sees
Searching " mug " (with a space before or after, e.g. from copy-paste) shows "No products match your search."; "mug" finds the Ceramic mug.

## Why it matters
Minor: shoppers may think the product doesn't exist. Also seen in run 1 (not filed then).

## Steps
1. Open /
2. Search for " mug " (leading and trailing space)

## Expected
Ceramic mug is listed (source: search-filter-sort: trims input)

## Actual
"No products match your search."

## Evidence
Verifier 1's session output (GET /?q=%20mug%20); evidence/002/t4-charter.txt

## Prompt for your coding agent
The search handler in server.js matches the raw `q`. Trim it before matching. Add a test that "/?q= mug " lists Ceramic mug.
