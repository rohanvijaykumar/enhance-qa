## What a user sees
At phone width, searching for one very long word (600 characters) makes the results page scroll sideways.

## Why it matters
Polish: the "Results for …" heading overflows instead of wrapping. Unlikely with real searches.

## Steps
1. At 390x844, open /
2. Search for "a" repeated 600 times

## Expected
No horizontal scroll: page scrollWidth equals 390 (source: A11Y-004 phone width)

## Actual
documentElement.scrollWidth 5186 vs clientWidth 390.

## Evidence
evidence/002/v1-c6-end.png · v1-c6.plan.json

## Prompt for your coding agent
The search-results heading echoes the query without wrapping. Add `overflow-wrap: anywhere` (or `word-break: break-word`) to it in static/style.css. Check at 390px wide.
