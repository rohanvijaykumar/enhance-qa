## What a user sees
After signing out, pressing the browser's Back button shows the Account page again, with the name, saved address and a "Sign out" button. Reloading then goes to sign in (the session did end: AUTH-010 and AUTH-017 pass).

## Why it matters
On a shared computer the next person can see the previous shopper's name and address.

## Steps
1. In Chrome, sign in and open /account
2. Click "Sign out"
3. Press Back

## Expected
The signed-in Account page is not shown again after sign out (source: accounts-auth: session end)

## Actual
The Account page ("Hi R2V1 Five", Sign out button) is shown from the browser cache. /account responses have no Cache-Control header.

## Evidence
evidence/002/v1-c5-end.png · v1-c5.plan.json · v1-c5.headers.txt

## Prompt for your coding agent
Signed-in pages (/account, /orders, /orders/:id, /cart, /checkout) are sent without Cache-Control, so the browser's back/forward cache shows them after sign out. Send `Cache-Control: no-store` on pages rendered for a signed-in user in server.js. Add a test that /account's response has `no-store`.
