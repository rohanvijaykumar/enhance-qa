## What a user sees
With 3 mugs in the cart, the mug's row shows $12.00 while the total says $36.00. In build 1 the row showed $36.00.

## Why it matters
Shoppers can't see what each line costs, and the row and total look inconsistent. New in build 5: it comes from the uncommitted edit to server.js's cart row (`money(prod.price * i.qty)` became `money(prod.price)`), made alongside the BUG-002 fix. The column header says "Price", so it may be intended.

## Steps
1. Sign in with an empty cart
2. Add Ceramic mug, set its quantity to 3, press "Update"
3. Look at the mug's row

## Expected
The row shows the line amount $36.00; Total $36.00; "Cart (3)" (source: CART-003; CART-002 with qty 2 → $24.00)

## Actual
Row shows "$12.00"; Total $36.00 and "Cart (3)" are right.

## Evidence
evidence/002/v1-c1t1.cart.html · v1-c1t2.cart.html · v1-c1t3.cart.html · t5-CART-003d-end.png

## Prompt for your coding agent
In server.js's /cart handler, the row's money cell now renders `money(prod.price)` (unit price). If the line amount was intended, restore `money(prod.price * i.qty)`; if a unit-price column was intended, add a separate line-amount column so each row's cost is visible. Add a test that qty 3 of a $12.00 item shows $36.00 on its row.
